Figure. Updated root–background segmentation and root-length agreement. (A) Actual 100-epoch loss histories. Updated V35 U-Net was continued on 1,048 native 250 training crops (784 existing-data and 264 additional-data crops); its selected checkpoint is epoch 91. DeepLabV3+ and SegFormer-B0 are retained baseline checkpoints, whose archived training histories used the earlier 244-file dataset (200 training/20 validation/24 legacy-test image files). Their histories are not new 310-file training runs. The V35 weighted training objective and unweighted monitoring objective have different weighting and are not directly comparable in absolute scale. (B) Pixel-level ROC curves recomputed on the identical 48 current monitoring parent images (3,145,728 pixels;153,297 root pixels), using native 250 windows, stride 188 and Hann probability blending for every model. (C) Measured versus predicted skeleton lengths on the same 310 parent grids:196 existing-data training files,66 additional training files and 48 monitoring files. Length is the number of skeleton pixels after Zhang skeletonization, without pruning, spatial resizing or physical-length conversion. Parent sizes are 256×256(existing) and 500×500(additional). The panels include marginal distributions, training-only ordinary least-squares fits, identity lines and prediction-minus-reference residuals. R² denotes 1−SSE/SST against the identity target; OLS R² measures a separate fitted linear relation within each partition; the displayed OLS line uses training parents only. (D) Row-normalized confusion matrices at the fixed 0.5 probability threshold for the same 48 monitoring parents. Each parent grid is scored once without repeatedly scoring its overlapping inference windows; parent images and temporal observations remain correlated. Spatially overlapping 250 px child views are not independent samples. The current dataset has no independent test split. Of the 48 monitoring files,44 had prior gradient-training exposure through inherited V35 weights. Baselines received different historical training data; these panels describe current development performance, not a controlled retraining benchmark or independent generalization. All RGB, reference masks and archived model weights were preserved.
